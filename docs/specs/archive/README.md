# Archive

**Nothing here is the current design.** The live specification is
[`../2026-08-28-carb3-site-energy-system-implementation.md`](../2026-08-28-carb3-site-energy-system-implementation.md)
and its four companions in [`../`](../). Those five documents are self-contained: they define
every entity, label and symbol they use, and **nothing in this directory is required reading
to understand the model.**

These documents are archived, **not retired**. One of them is the COMIT-parity baseline, and
the chain of custody that makes any result defensible runs through it in two hops:

```
   coupled-off COMIT  ──V1──►  baseline specification  ──V1b──►  the live specification
                                        (here)                        (../)
```

Neither hop may be skipped, so the baseline must stay readable, citable and runnable. It
just no longer sits in the main reading path.

## Reading a `§` reference across the two specifications

**Section numbers are deliberately aligned.** Where both documents define an entity it sits
at the same number in both — §3.11 is `premise_measured_emissions` in each. Where the live
specification replaced a baseline entity, the replacement took the original's number:
`carrier` is §3.4 because `commodity` was, and `unit` is §3.5 because `technology` was.

So a `§3.x` reference means the same thing in either document, and the two can be read side
by side without translation. That alignment is load-bearing for V1b and should survive any
future renumbering.

The one place they diverge is §3.3: `activity_process_energy_profile` (a vector split by
fuel) became `activity_process_duty_profile` (a split by duty and grade). Same slot, same
job, different decomposition — and the baseline's table survives as V1b's parity target
rather than as an input.

## What still points here

- `docs/notes/examples/build_interface_docs.py` and `build_spec_flow_diagram.py` read the
  baseline specification from this directory and regenerate `interfaces/` and `diagrams/`
  into it.
- The live specification links here twice, both times for the parity chain: at its §1.1 and
  at §10.2.
- `docs/notes/11_sector_coverage_and_carb3_mapping.md` cites the baseline.

Moving or renaming anything here without updating those will break `make docs-check`.

## Known defects, recorded rather than fixed

The frozen documents contain three known errors. They are listed in the
[review log](2026-09-01-carb3-site-energy-system-review-log.md) with enough detail to work
around. They are not corrected, because correcting a frozen parity baseline invalidates it
rather than improving it, and nothing in the live specification depends on them.
